About The Signal

News, filed as it happens.

The Signal is a small, independent-minded newsroom covering World, Technology, Business, Sports and Entertainment. This particular edition is a coursework build for CIIS 332: Web Application Development — every story on the site is sample copy, and every photo is a labeled placeholder waiting to be replaced.

How we work

Our reporters file short, dated stories the moment something is confirmed, then follow up as details firm up — hence the wire-ticker running across the top of every page. It's a nod to old telex newsrooms, where the first version of a story was rarely the last.

Each section is edited independently, but every desk follows the same house rules: attribute what you can, flag what you can't, and never bury the lede.
